What we knew

An Advance offers a business a loan based on its predicted future sales, with a fixed fee. Merchants pay it back automatically with a percentage of their sales, and the percentage depends on a series of factors.

Potential problems

  • Users do not find the CTA on the landing page
  • It is unclear how repayment works
  • Users may want to choose different payback times

Concepts

How might we redesign our flows to help users understand the product better?
Concept workshop
Three concepts: a slider with different repayment options, a fixed offers one pager and a chatbot

User research

Ten merchants in the UK and ten in Sweden.

Interviews with merchants

What worked

  • User interaction is important, so let users play around
  • Some friction is needed
  • They appreciate how quick and easy it is to take an Advance
  • Friendliness is key

What did not

  • Chatbots can be perceived as a scam, like an SMS loan
  • Users struggle to find the CTA on the landing page
  • Users still do not understand how the fixed fee is calculated or how repayment works
  • It is unclear if users want to choose different payback times

Iterate

How might we make the landing page CTA visible?
How might we use the research learnings to be more clear about how the product works?
