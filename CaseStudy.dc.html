Case study, 2019

iZettle Capital: Defaults

Understanding why small business owners fell behind on loan repayments, and designing a way to keep them on track.

RoleSenior UX designer
Core teamTwo designers and one PM
CollaboratorsAbout 30 specialists across Credit, Machine Learning, Risk, Support, Marketing and Engineering
Scope0 to 1, from discovery to delivery
The problem
14%

of iZettle Advance loan customers underperformed in the repayment flow. They repaid late or not at all.

The result
~20%

fewer defaults after the new repayment experience launched.

The challenge

The team had to understand the root causes of late and missed repayments, then find ways to reduce them. Credit, Risk, Machine Learning, Support, Marketing and Engineering each saw a different part of the problem.

The question

How might we reduce the current loan default rate of 14%?

What was at stake

When a merchant fell behind on repayments, iZettle first tried to agree a payment plan. If that failed, the case was handed to an external collections partner and iZettle took the loss. That loss was as big as the salaries of everyone working in Capital.

1. LoaniZettle gives a merchant a Cash Advance
2. Warning signRepayments are not going as predicted
3. Pre-collectioniZettle contacts the merchant to set up a payment plan
4. CollectionThe merchant breaks the plan and iZettle offers a new one
5. LossThe case goes to an external collections partner and iZettle takes the loss

Solutions and priorities

A second workshop asked how we could make it easier for the customer to repay. Ideas for each theme were then placed on a value and effort grid. Design, Credit and backend solutions were sorted into separate backlogs, and the design solution that ranked highest was the traffic light.

Solution ideas for each theme
Ideas placed by value and effort

The solution

A traffic light banner in the loan repayment dashboard. It changes colour and message based on repayment status, and shows whether a merchant is falling behind, why, and what to do to keep up.

My role

  • Facilitated cross-functional workshops and aligned the disciplines involved.
  • Structured the end-to-end user journey map so teams could find opportunities grounded in real user problems.
  • Led testing, prioritisation and design of the final experience.
  • Drove the work from discovery through delivery as 0 to 1 product work.

Impact

Defaults reduced by around 20%.
Risk and Credit reviewed their ways of working based on what the workshops uncovered.
Stakeholders recognised Design as a strategic partner.
Teams working on iZettle Capital got to know each other and collaborated better.
A broader journey map became the basis for the following year's (2020) roadmap.
