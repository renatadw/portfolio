How we worked

We had to launch an all in one device that helps sellers become truly mobile. The Hardware team was incredibly senior, with a lot of experience building new hardware and running and compiling results from pilots. My part was helping them organise their backlog and timelines, building up their confidence to share the project's findings across the company, and making sure we had good support for hardware accessibility.

Sound design

The Terminal sounds were designed by Plan8. Together we picked twelve moments where sound helps, like a confirmed payment, a declined card or a locked session, so sellers and buyers can follow along without reading the screen.

Today

Terminal is still one of the main devices PayPal sells. As Zettle became PayPal Point of Sale, it was renamed PayPal Terminal, and it keeps the same all in one idea.
